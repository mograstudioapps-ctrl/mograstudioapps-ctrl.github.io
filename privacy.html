Privacy policy

Effective 16 November 2026. Applies to the Android games Gulal Sort and Jaali Blocks by Mogra Studio ("we").

In short: we have no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Your progress stays on your phone. The only data shared with others is what Google's advertising service (AdMob) uses to show ads, and in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland it asks for your consent first.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store your name, email, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information. The games do not have sign-in or accounts and do not use analytics tools.

2. Information stored on your phone

Your level progress, stars, daily-puzzle streak, chosen pot or piece style and settings (sound, vibration, colour symbols) are saved only on your device so you can continue where you left off. We cannot see this data. If Android backup is switched on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account; this is handled by Google under its own privacy policy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The games show ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some levels, and an optional ad you can choose to watch for extra help. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and limit how often you see the same ad, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a consent message from Google appears before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings > Ad choices in the game.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Android settings (Privacy > Ads).

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Ratings

A game may show Google Play's own rating window. Any rating or review you give is handled by Google Play; we do not receive personal information from it.

5. Permissions

The games use internet and network-state access only to load ads. They do not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

6. Children

The games are made for a general audience aged 13 and over and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Keeping and deleting data

We hold no data about you on any server. To delete everything a game stored on your phone, clear the game's storage in Android settings or uninstall the game. For data used by Google for advertising, use your Android ad settings and Google's privacy controls.

8. Changes

If the games start handling data differently, we will update this page and its effective date before the change.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: mograstudio.apps@gmail.com
